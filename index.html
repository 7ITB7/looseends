What to do with an open one

  1. Double-check the index yourself. Releases are sometimes indexed under a different name, or a later assignment moved the mortgage to another lender.
  2. Open mortgages before a later sale are the strongest leads: the loan was almost certainly paid at closing but the lender never recorded the release.
  3. Bring it to the closing attorney or title company on the next deal, or to the current owner. Getting a release recorded is legal work: the attorney requests it from the lender (or its successor), and state law gives owners remedies when lenders don't release paid-off mortgages.
  4. You find and document; the attorney handles it. Don't give legal advice yourself.
